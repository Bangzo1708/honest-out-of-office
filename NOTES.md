# NOTES — The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office

## 3 varian kandidat (semua satu kalimat, ≤200 karakter)

**V1 — 190 karakter (DIPILIH):**
> I'm away until Monday, and honestly, your email will join a queue of 400 unread messages I'm politely pretending doesn't exist — thank you for your patience, I'll get to it as soon as I can.

**V2 — 167 karakter:**
> I'm out of office until Monday, taking the break I kept postponing because the inbox never stops — your message is safe with me, and I'll reply properly once I'm back.

**V3 — 179 karakter:**
> Thank you for your email — I'm away until Monday, resting the way I should have months ago, and your message will be answered in the order it arrived, which is to say, eventually.

## Penilaian (kriteria juri, urut penting)

| Kriteria | V1 | V2 | V3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1) Sangat jujur DAN tetap sopan | Kuat — jujur soal antrean, tetap ucap terima kasih | Sedang — jujur soal cuti tertunda | Kuat — jujur soal antrean |
| (2) Mengakui hal yang hanya berani dibilang privat | **Paling kuat** — "400 unread messages I'm politely pretending doesn't exist" adalah pengakuan spesifik yang karyawan sungguhan takkan tulis di auto-reply biasa | Sedang — cuti yang terus ditunda, umum | Kuat — "answered... eventually" |
| (3) Nada hangat & profesional | Hangat — "thank you for your patience" | Hangat | Hangat |
| (4) Satu kalimat ≤200 karakter, tidak berjejal | 190 karakter, mengalir | 167 karakter, mengalir | 179 karakter, mengalir |
| (5) Turn of phrase layak dikutip | **"politely pretending doesn't exist"** — frasa paling tajam & mudah diingat | Lemah — "inbox never stops" klise | Kuat — "which is to say, eventually" |

## Alasan pilihan V1

- Paling nempel tema: pengakuan paling konkret dan paling "painfully honest" — angka 400 + pura-pura tidak ada adalah hal yang benar-benar hanya diucapkan secara pribadi.
- Paling orisinal: "politely pretending doesn't exist" menghindari klise ("catching up on emails", "will get back to you soon") yang hampir pasti dipakai banyak entry lain.
- Paling berkesan di pandangan pertama: kontras "politely pretending" (sopan + jujur dalam satu frasa) langsung terasa — persis yang dicari juri di kriteria 1 dan 5.
- V3 kalah tipis: "eventually" bagus, tapi "resting the way I should have months ago" terasa seperti keluhan umum; V2 paling lemah karena tidak ada pengakuan spesifik soal inbox.
